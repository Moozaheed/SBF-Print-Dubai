## 2. Accepted Payment Gateways & BNPL Options

SBF Print And Design accepts 7 flexible payment methods across B2C retail and B2B corporate customers:

| Payment Method | Provider / Type | Description | Target Customer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visa** | Credit & Debit Card | Secure online card payment processing | Retail & B2B Clients |
| **Mastercard** | Credit & Debit Card | Secure online card payment processing | Retail & B2B Clients |
| **Apple Pay** | Express Mobile Wallet | One-tap touch/face ID checkout on iOS devices | Mobile B2C Buyers |
| **Google Pay** | Express Mobile Wallet | One-tap express checkout on Android devices | Mobile B2C Buyers |
| **Tabby (BNPL)** | Buy Now Pay Later | Split print order in 4 equal interest-free installments | Retail & Small Business |
| **Tamara (BNPL)** | Buy Now Pay Later | Split print order in 3 or 4 interest-free monthly payments | Retail & Small Business |
| **Bank Transfer / Cash on Pickup** | B2B Corporate Credit | Pay via UAE bank transfer or cash upon press pickup | Corporate B2B Clients |
